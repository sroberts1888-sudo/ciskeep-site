Common questions

SubbieRecord FAQ

What is SubbieRecord?

SubbieRecord is an independent Android record-keeping app for UK subcontractors who want a straightforward way to track Construction Industry Scheme payments and deductions, contractors and tax-year totals.

Does SubbieRecord send my records to a server?

No. Normal SubbieRecord records are stored locally on your device. Files only leave the app when you deliberately export, back up or share them.

Can I use SubbieRecord part-way through a tax year?

Yes. Opening balances let you bring existing contractor totals into the correct tax year without creating artificial payment entries.

Does SubbieRecord support reverse charge VAT?

Yes. SubbieRecord can record normal VAT and Domestic Reverse Charge information alongside payment records.

How much does SubbieRecord Premium cost?

The free plan supports up to five payment records. Premium is £3.99 per month or £29.99 per year and unlocks unlimited payment records plus PDF and CSV reporting.

Is SubbieRecord an HMRC or UK Government app?

No. SubbieRecord is an independent record-keeping tool from PocketForge Studio and is not affiliated with or endorsed by HMRC or the UK Government.
