Common questions

CISKeep FAQ

What is CISKeep?

CISKeep is an Android record-keeping app for UK subcontractors who want a straightforward way to track Construction Industry Scheme payments, deductions, contractors and tax-year totals.

Does CISKeep send my records to a server?

No. Normal CISKeep records are stored locally on your device. Files only leave the app when you deliberately export, back up or share them.

Can I use CISKeep part-way through a tax year?

Yes. Opening balances let you bring existing contractor totals into the correct tax year without creating artificial payment entries.

Does CISKeep support reverse charge VAT?

Yes. CISKeep can record normal VAT and Domestic Reverse Charge information alongside CIS payment records.
